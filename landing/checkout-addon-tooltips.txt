Section: Weekly Rewards with 80% Reward Split
========================================================================

On "instant" use this:

Weekly Rewards with 80% Split

Keep the same 80% reward share as the default Bi-Weekly cycle, requested every 7 calendar days instead of 14. Minimum reward $100.

Payouts after 5 valid trading days (0.5% vs start-of-day equity).

Cannot be combined with On Demand Rewards or the 90% Reward Split.
