## Four things that gate any spend

Meta treats proprietary trading as a **restricted financial product**. Copy is not what gets accounts banned — the funnel and the destination are. Details in [01-CAMPAIGN.md](01-CAMPAIGN.md).

1. **Financial-services authorisation** for every country you target
2. **Financial products and services** Special Ad Category selected
3. **Bridge page** as the ad destination — never a checkout link
4. **No earnings content** on that destination (reward calculator, "$1M / 90%" headline, dollar certificates)

## Claim rules that never bend

| Product truth | Never say |
|---|---|
| Static max loss is **2-Step only** | Instant + static, or Instant + fee refund |
| Instant uses a **trailing** max loss and refunds nothing | "Instant is the easy one" |
| Fee back on first reward = **evaluations only** | "Fee back" on any Instant creative |
| **80%** is the bi-weekly base cycle | "Up to 90%" as the standard split |
| Weekly 70% and on-demand 90% are **add-ons** | "You pick the cycle" |
| "**Most** rewards process in under 24 hours" | "You get paid in 24 hours" |
| Simulated evaluation, demo accounts | "CFD trading", "get funded" as a promise |

## What we sell in this campaign

**Instant $5,000 — $72** (no fee refund) and **1-Step $5,000 — $36** (fee back on first reward), with code **VERO35**. One code, never stacked.

Instant at $25k and above is at or below break-even, so it stays out of every ad, pin, and carousel.

**No prices spoken in any video.** Numbers live on statics, in ad copy, and at checkout, which keeps every clip usable after a price change.
