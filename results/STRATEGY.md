# What Verodus should do

Same industry-calibrated book for every firm (7% Pro / 22% Semi-skilled / 26% Average / 28% Aggressive / 17% Lottery). A standard FTMO-style 2-step lands at **P1 22% / funded 13% / P(pay) 13% / year-1 4%** — inside the Track360/FPFX band (P1 25–35%, funded 5–14%, ever-paid ~7%, long-term 1–3%). Instant P(pay) here is *first-payout eligibility*; year-1 (~7% on Verodus Instant) is the figure that matches the 4–6% “sustained Instant” disclosures.

**Headline:** do not race Maven/Alpha on sticker. Verodus already prints on every eval SKU. The only hole is Instant $25k+ (same hole every Instant peer has). Fix Instant with **rules + a payout cap**, not a price cut. On evals, **raise prices toward the mid-tier** and **close the Lite funded-10% hole**.

**Do this**

5. **Do not cut VERO35.** $72 vs list $110 is the shopper language. Cut list if you must, not the sale.

**Do not** loosen daily to 4% or drop consistency to 30% to “look easier.” Instant demand is price + speed, not room. Loosening Instant is how Alpha/FP lose money at $50k+.

## 7. Competitive posture (how to sell it)

- **vs FTMO:** “Same 2-step room on Pro at ~70% of their $100k fee. 1-Step has 4% daily (they have 3%) and a refund (their 1-Step does not).”
- **vs FundingPips:** “We do not sell a $23 6/6 Pro that dies on 3% daily. Lite is the cheap 2-step with 4% daily and an 8% floor on both sides.”
- **vs Alpha Instant $40:** “We are not the $40 Instant. Those books go red at $25k. Ours is priced to pay.”
- **vs Maven $18:** Ignore. Lowest-price 2-step is a 2026 graveyard SKU.
- **vs Hola:** Closest rule-for-rule peer. Beat them on 1-Step price ($49 vs $47 is a wash — win on refund + 4% daily) and on Instant $5k honesty.

Affiliate take is 10–15% of fee in this vertical. The 40% column in `industry_skus.csv` is the all-in target **before** affiliate; after a 12% CPA you want sale ≥ 40% column. Instant $5k is already there ($73). Evals have room to raise and still clear 40% after CPA.

## 8. What not to do

- Do not launch a 3-step to match Maven $17.
- Do not drop Instant consistency to “get more passers.” You are not paid on passers; you are paid on people who never collect.
- Do not add news-trading bans as a margin tool. They are a support cost, not a DD filter.
- Do not copy FundedNext Instant (no daily, 6% trail, 53% P(pay) in this book) unless you also cap the account at $20k and charge $99.
- Do not wait for CRM to “prove” the Lite hole. The Aug 15 peer PDFs and this book agree: 10% funded after 8% eval is the SKU that breaks when the book fattens.
