## What the street is doing

- **Holding:** The5ers, Fintokei, For Traders, Goat CFD, FXIFY evals include weekend. FTMO / Alpha / Maven / Instant Funding sell Swing as a **SKU or one toggle**. Charging 12%+12% and bundling at 20% is in-family with Instant Funding’s news+weekend addon and FTMO Swing (~10–15% SKU premium + lower leverage).
- **90%:** Published fees cluster **10–20%** of the challenge (Alpha ~10%, BG 15%, FXIFY / BrightFunded 20%, Goat futures 90% 20%, FN 95% **25–30%**). FundedNext on-demand **+5% with 95%** is an outlier — do not match.
- **Speed:** BG eval 7-day **+15%**. FXIFY biweekly **+5%**. BrightFunded weekly **+25%**. FN biweekly **+15–25%**. FundingPips / Hola make faster cycles a **worse split**, not a fee.
- **90% + speed bundle:** Only BG publishes a real save (15+15 → **25%**). FXIFY stacks 20+5 with no save. FN’s +5% on-demand already includes 95%.
- **Qty:** Only BG has a shopper-facing 4-pack ladder (25/30/35/40). FN Double Up is +40% now for a 2nd account later. For Traders uses BOGO. Instant Funding uses points on the next order.
