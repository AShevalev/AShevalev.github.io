## How to read this

Premium / trustworthy / established / desirable is not a copy problem. The firms that feel that way have **proof, a story, a product ladder, and a public record**. Verodus already has a cleaner honesty stance than most of the category (simulated evaluation, no deposits, no client funds). It does not yet have the **receipts** those firms put on the first screen.

The wording brief is necessary. It is not sufficient. After it ships, Verodus will still look like a well-designed 2026 launch — not like a firm a cautious trader would pick over FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, or Alpha Capital.

## 1. What the category actually sells in 2026

Every serious CFD/forex prop site now runs the same machine. Differences are in **proof density** and **which anxiety they remove**.

| Layer | What traders scan in 10 seconds | Industry pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Hero stance | “Will they pay, and how fast?” | Founded-year + payout total + review widget + one guarantee |
| Product | “Can I start today, or do I have to pass?” | Instant + 1-Step + 2-Step, often 3-Step / Zero / Labs |
| Economics | “What’s the cheapest $100k and the best split?” | Entry from ~$29–$79; splits 80% default, 90–100% at the top |
| Rules | “Will my style survive?” | News, weekend, EAs, trailing vs static, consistency/best-day |
| Proof | “Have real people been paid?” | Named certificates, YouTube, live reward feeds, Discord |
| Institution | “Is this a company or a landing page?” | Offices, staff counts, founders, broker affiliate, awards |

**Two marketing tribes**

1. **Establishment (FTMO, The5ers, CTI, Fintokei, Topstep).** Years, founders with names, awards, academy, “career not lottery.” Pricing is higher. Copy is calmer.
2. **Velocity (FundedNext, FundingPips, Blue Guardian, Goat, AquaFunded, FXIFY).** Promo codes, 24h guarantees with a penalty, $X00M paid, 100% split, scaling to $2–4M, Discord screenshots.

Verodus currently sits in a **third tribe that does not yet exist at scale**: simulated-honest + premium-quiet. Alpha Capital is the closest peer (UK firm, simulated language, Qualified Analyst, group/broker story). Alpha still beats Verodus on volume (`1.2M+ traders`, `$100M+` performance fees, interviews, five platforms, Alpha Prime).

## 3. Challenge and commercial patterns Verodus is measured against

### 3.1 Product grid (what “complete” looks like)

| Product | Typical rules | Who uses it | Verodus live |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instant / Zero / Direct | No profit target; tighter DD (often 3/6 or 4/6 trailing); consistency 15–25% | Traders who failed evals or want cashflow | **Yes.** 3% daily HWM, 6% trailing **never locks**, 20% Best Day, 1:30, $5k–$100k, Instant fees **not refundable** |
| 1-Step | 8–10% target; 4–6% daily; 6–10% max | Fast qualification | **Yes.** 10% target, 4% daily, 6% hybrid trail-then-lock, 50% Best Day, $5k–$200k, fee refund on first payout |
| 2-Step Lite | 8%/5%, easier DD | Volume / beginners | **Yes.** 8%/5%, 4%/8% static, 5 min days |
| 2-Step Pro / Classic | 10%/5%, 5%/10% | FTMO-shaped | **Yes.** 10%/5%, 5%/10% static, 5 min days |
| 3-Step / Bootcamp | Tiny targets, cheap | Maven, Fintokei StartTrader, Aqua | **No** |
| Futures book | Separate rules, payout caps | FundedNext, Blue Guardian, FXIFY, Topstep | **No** (not required for CFD positioning) |
| Scaling / Prime / VIP | +25% size or $2–4M cap, split ↑ | Almost every top firm | **No public scaling ladder.** Homepage `$1M Max Capital` is a **combined cap**, not a career path |
| Loyalty / academy / competitions | Points, merch, free accounts | CTI, Fintokei, FXIFY, BrightFunded | **Free Trial only.** FAQ mentions Discord; homepage does not prove a community |

Verodus’s **four-model CFD grid is commercially complete** for a new CFD firm. The missing commercial objects are **scaling**, a **payout guarantee with a penalty**, and a **visible $100k price** that is competitive after promo.

### 3.2 Instant vs 1-Step vs 2-Step — how Verodus’s rules feel

Live Instant ([instant.html](https://www.verodus.com/instant.html)):

- No target, no min days — **on-trend with Goat, Blue Guardian Instant, FundingPips Zero, Maven Instant.**
- 6% trailing that **never locks** is **stricter** than 1-Step’s hybrid lock and stricter than many Instant books that eventually static-lock.
- 20% Best Day is **tight** (Maven Instant uses 20%; Aqua/FXIFY Instant often 15–30%; 2-Step Pro at Verodus has **no** Best Day on eval). This is the Instant “gotcha” traders will hunt for on Trustpilot.
- Leverage **1:30 FX** vs **1:100** at FTMO / The5ers / FundingPips / CTI. Instant feels smaller and more constrained.
- Homepage Instant table still shows a **$200,000** column and fees. Plan FAQ and Instant rules: **no $200k Instant SKU.** That is a live integrity bug (wording brief defers it).

Live 1-Step: 10% / 4% / 6% hybrid / 50% Best Day / no min days. Competitive with The5ers-style 1-Steps. Unique hybrid DD needs a **diagram**, not only text.

Live 2-Step Lite (8/5, 4/8) and Pro (10/5, 5/10) are recognizable FTMO-family products. 5 min days per phase is standard. Fee refund on first payout matches FTMO/Alpha expectation.

Reward cycles: Weekly 70% (addon) / Bi-weekly 80% / On-Demand 90% (addon). This is the FundingPips idea, **without** 100% monthly, without publishing **average first reward** by size (FundingPips: “$5,020 avg first rewards” on $100k).

## 4. Verodus live site — what it is, and how it holds up

### 4.1 What already works (keep)

- **Honesty on the first screen:** “no deposits, no client funds, no brokerage” plus simulated language in Why Verodus. FTMO, Alpha, For Traders, and Fintokei do this; most velocity firms still say “our capital / get funded” and bury sim in the footer. **Do not abandon this.** It is the only durable brand difference Verodus has.
- **Four-step process** that includes KYC before Qualified. Adult. Matches how payouts actually work.
- **Rule pages** for Instant and 1-Step are unusually specific (HWM examples, Best Day as non-breach, restricted practices). This is closer to FTMO Trading Objectives than to Goat’s FAQ shrug.
- **Free Trial** in the hero — FTMO/Fintokei/CTI pattern. Keep.
- **Rise + crypto** rails — table stakes, correctly shown.
- **Dual interface, one account** — good operational idea. Naming “Platform 5 / TradeHub” instead of MT5 / TradingView-style is a **trust tax** (see §5).
- **Reward-cycle math** and calculator — good. Calculator should not be the only “how much can I make” object; FundingPips uses **real first-reward averages**.
- **Global country strip** — visually right; **3,000+ traders is an accurate count** (operator confirmation). Keep it. Pairing it with 175 countries is fine; do not demote the stat.

### 4.2 Trust-breaking issues (fix before more copy)

These are more damaging than any remaining adjective.

**A. Certificate tiles — keep; add IDs so they are harder to confuse with another firm’s marketing**

The homepage certificates are **legitimate Verodus payouts** (operator confirmation). Keep the carousel. Do not hide the section.

A stranger comparing sites the same day can still see overlapping **first names and amounts** on Goat Funded Trader (e.g. Yassir $466.28, Rehman $2,925.26, Joe/Jose $9,401.23). That is a **perception** problem, not a reason to take Verodus proof down. Strengthen each tile with a unique Verodus certificate ID, date, and country. Do **not** add payout rail (Rise / crypto). Hide only a duplicate tile **on the Verodus carousel itself**.

**B. Testimonials are generic and unpriced**

Carlos / Amani / A.K. / Li W. praise “clear rules.” No amounts, no dates, no video, no Trustpilot embed. FTMO puts **YouTube + $114,907**. For Traders puts **video + unique certificates**. FundingPips puts a **12-day journey from $422 fee to $4,050**. Unpriced quotes read as placeholder.

**C. Trustpilot “4.5 / 5” with no volume**

Homepage states Rated 4.5 / 5 and links. The wording brief keeps this. Competitors put **review count** next to the score (FundedNext 73k, FundingPips 66k, FTMO tens of thousands). A 4.5 with no N looks like a badge, not a market. Independently, Verodus does **not** appear on PropFirmsCompare’s 2026 ranked table at all. That is the discovery problem.

**D. “Launching in 2026” on the blog**

[`best-simulated-prop-firm-2026.html`](https://www.verodus.com/best-simulated-prop-firm-2026.html) says Verodus is the premier firm **launching in 2026**, uses future tense (“will refund,” “will emphasize”), omits Instant, quotes 10%/5%/10%/5% as if that were the whole product, and titles itself “best.” Established firms never narrate themselves in the future tense. This page currently **proves newness**. Wording brief: fix numbers or unpublish; do not rewrite. **Recommendation beyond the brief:** unpublish until it can be a sourced comparison, not a launch manifesto.

**E. Discretionary payouts (brief already patches this)**

Live FAQ “Is Verodus legitimate?”: performance rewards are **discretionary**. Live Terms § Performance Reward Rates: **discretionary and are not liabilities**. Live About: “internal evaluation outcomes.” Competitors who feel premium say the **rule**, then the **exception** (breach, KYC, prohibited practices). FTMO/Alpha/For Traders still disclose simulation; they do not lead with “we may choose not to pay.”

**F. Legal identity split**

Terms: Verodus L.L.C.-FZ; payments Verodus Capital Inc. Privacy data controller: **Verodus LLC**. About does not state a registration, office, or company number. Goat, BrightFunded, FTMO, and For Traders put **legal name + city + company number** in the footer. Premium is boring specificity.

**G. Founder proof is intramural**

Kim Chen and Alexander Vladimirovich exist in Read Bio modals (brief: keep). A stranger still cannot verify school, employer, years, or LinkedIn. FTMO puts **named founders + Prague HQ + 250 staff + awards**. Fintokei puts **CEO name on the homepage**. Alpha films traders; For Traders films the **office**. Bios without an external graph do not create “established.”
