**Force the word “profits.”** In this business it can mean three different things:

| If he means | Verodus position |
|---|---|
| % of **net evaluation fees** after refunds and chargebacks (Instant / 1-Step / Lite he actually sent) | Take. 15–25%. Unique code. 90 days. |
| % of **trader payouts / “profits” on qualified accounts** | Refuse. That is the reward pool. Directing buyers who later get paid should not tax Verodus’s payout line. |

Kim’s “directing customers to us” is the right activity. Pay him only on **tagged net fees**. No code, no percentage.
