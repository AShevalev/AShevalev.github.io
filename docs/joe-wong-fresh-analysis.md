## 8. Risk to Verodus if you say yes to the email as written

1. **False halo.** “Official partner of a Saudi-government / OHKF competition” will be read as Verodus’s claim. None of it is evidenced.
2. **SFC adjacency.** MoniMath already confuses Type 2 with Type 4 and “approval” with “partners.” A joint page would import that mess.
3. **Product mismatch.** Instant is simulated evaluation. His page says “funded traders.” His email says order-flow platform. Customers and Trustpilot will not parse the difference if he writes the copy.
4. **Template risk.** Next partner gets the same email with Verodus’s name left in, the way Bookmap’s name was left in.
5. **PropTrade fork.** If you give him a logo and no contract, he can still launch PropTrade elsewhere and tell the market Verodus “partnered.” Lock language: originator, not owner; no SFC; no OHKF; no Saudi.
