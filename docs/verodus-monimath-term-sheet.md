# Verodus × MoniMath — Discussion Term Sheet (Draft)

**Status:** Discussion draft only. Not an offer. Subject to diligence, counsel, and privacy-policy alignment.  
**Date:** 20 August 2026

## 1. Parties

- **Verodus:** Verodus LLC / operating affiliate (evaluation CRM, simulated challenges, risk, KYC, payouts).
- **Counterparty:** TradeMath / MoniMath / MoniMath Capital legal entity to be named, with Joe Wong as commercial lead.

## 2. What this is (and is not)

This is a **paid data license + Asia origination** arrangement, with an optional white-label of Verodus evaluation rails.

This is **not** a merger, not an exclusive strategic JV, not a fund partnership, and not a commitment to improve Verodus pass rates.

## 3. Commercial layers

### A. Data license (cash)

Verodus licenses **anonymized, non-identifiable** trader datasets from its CRM:

| SKU | Contents | Pilot | Year 1 (indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lifecycle | Pass/fail/breach labels, drawdown envelope, program type, size band, regime tags. No fills. | Included | Included |
| 2. Behavioral features | Sizing volatility, news-window flags, consistency / best-day ratios, session mix, EA vs manual marker. No raw tickets. | Not in pilot | Included |
| 3. Survival | Evaluation behavior vs later funded survival / payout / breach. | Not available | Optional add-on |

**Pilot fee:** USD 25,000 for 90 days, SKU 1 only, row cap to be set, credited 50% to Year 1 if converted.  
**Year 1 license:** USD 10,000 per month (SKU 1+2), 12-month term, volume ratchet as Verodus accounts grow.  
**SKU 3:** +USD 5,000 per month after SKU 1+2 are live and diligence is complete.

Verodus **owns** the data. License is non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, purpose-limited (internal TAMS training only). No PII, KYC, device, IP, wallet, or copy-trading of named accounts.

### B. Origination / exposure (volume)

MoniMath originates buyers to **standard Verodus products** (unchanged rules) via tracked links.

- **20% of net evaluation fees** on tagged checkouts, paid monthly after refunds, fraud, and chargebacks.
- No share of funded-account performance rewards.
- No paid ads on the Verodus brand. 30-day last-click. Clawback for self-dealing and abuse.
- Co-marketing limited to agreed Asia landing pages. No SFC / Jane Street / “Verodus-backed fund” claims without written approval.

### C. Optional: PropTrade on Verodus rails

If MoniMath runs a funded-trader / PropTrade program, it runs **exclusively on Verodus CRM, risk, KYC, and payouts** for 24 months.

- Verodus retains **80%** of challenge GMV; MoniMath **20%** origination.
- Same hard rules as Verodus public products unless a separately priced SKU is agreed.
- If they refuse this layer, they do not get SKU 2 or 3.

### D. Optional: academy add-on

TradeMath coaching sold as an **optional** checkout add-on. 50/50 GMV split. Never required to start or pass a Verodus evaluation.

## 4. Non-compete / use limits

While the license is in force and for 24 months after:

- No competing retail evaluation / prop-challenge product except as a Verodus white-label.
- No use of Verodus data to solicit Verodus traders.
- No residual model rights after termination unless a buyout is paid.
- Audit rights; deletion within 15 days of termination.
