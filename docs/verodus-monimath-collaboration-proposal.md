## 1. Executive recommendation

**Do the deal. Do not do *their* deal.**

Joe Wong (Hong Kong; LinkedIn: [joe-wong-55b3ab262](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joe-wong-55b3ab262)) is co-founder of MoniMath / TradeMath. He is not the comedian Huang Xi and not Fed Guy Joseph Wang. Public materials show an early-stage marketing vehicle that *wants* three things Verodus already has:

1. **Labeled trader behavioral data** at prop-firm scale (what TAMS is advertised as needing).
2. **A working evaluation CRM / challenge stack** (they announced a “MoniMath PropTrade Program” for January 2026 and still have no comparable public product).
3. **A real trader base** they can point to (their site claims “10K+ global members”; Joe’s own LinkedIn posts on MoniMath typically get 2–5 reactions).

Verodus should treat this as a **seller’s market**. The scarce asset is Verodus data + infrastructure. Joe’s scarce assets are **Asia access, a Chinese-language / HK network, and a willingness to originate traffic**. Those are real, but they are not worth giving him raw data, exclusivity, or a competing white-label without cash.

**Recommended structure (in this order):**

| Layer | What Verodus sells | How Verodus gets paid | Effect on failure-fee economics |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Data license | Anonymized, labeled behavioral / lifecycle datasets from the CRM | Setup fee + annual license (and/or revenue share on any fund/product that uses the data) | Neutral to positive. Does not raise pass rates. |
| B. Asia distribution / affiliate | Verodus challenges sold into HK / Greater China / APAC under a co-brand or MoniMath referral code | 15–25% of **originated** challenge-fee net, paid after chargebacks | Positive. More buyers, same rules, same failure rate. |
| C. Optional white-label engine | MoniMath PropTrade runs **on Verodus rails** (CRM, risk, payouts, KYC) | 70–85% of challenge GMV stays with Verodus; they keep 15–30% for origination | Positive if volume is incremental. Dangerous if they clone and leave. |
| D. Optional education upsell | TradeMath academy as a **paid add-on**, never a required path | 40–60% of education GMV sold through Verodus checkout | Positive. Does not touch the core high-failure product. |

**Do not** make education mandatory, do not improve pass rates as a partnership KPI, and do not give them exclusive data without a large minimum guarantee plus a non-compete.

Walk away if they will not name the SFC partner, will not pay for data, or want the CRM feed in order to launch a competing evaluation product off Verodus rails.

## 4. Hard constraint: current privacy policy vs “we can sell the data”

The user note is that the **backend CRM is contractually allowed to sell data**. That is necessary but not sufficient.

The **public** [Privacy Policy](https://www.verodus.com/privacy.html) (effective 21 February 2026) currently says:

- “Verodus **does not use trading behavior data to replicate, commercialize, or exploit user trading strategies externally**.”
- “Trader P&L, trading strategies, and behavior data may be shared **internally only** with risk management and compliance personnel… **This information is not shared externally**.”
- “Verodus **does not sell personal information** in exchange for monetary compensation.”
- Anonymized / aggregated statistics (leaderboards, performance metrics) may be published.

So: CRM vendor permission ≠ public legal permission. Selling *identifiable* trades, P&L, or “this user’s strategy” would contradict the current policy and is a GDPR / UAE PDPL / CCPA problem.

**What Verodus *can* sell, if counsel amends the policy and the dataset is properly anonymized:**

- Aggregated statistics.
- Irreversibly anonymized, non-reidentifiable feature tables (account hashed, no names, no KYC, no wallet, no IP, k-anonymity / suppression of rare strategy fingerprints).
- Research labels: pass/fail, breach type, holding-time buckets, sizing-volatility scores, regime tags.

**What Verodus should not sell even after a policy update:**

- PII, KYC, biometrics, payment data, device IDs, IPs.
- Raw order-by-order tapes that can reconstruct a named trader’s edge.
- Anything that lets MoniMath **copy-trade** Verodus’s best funded accounts.
- Anything that lets them rebuild Verodus’s risk engine and launch a clone.

**Gating work before any data leaves the building:**

1. Counsel memo: CRM contract vs privacy policy vs Terms vs GDPR/PDPL/CCPA “sale” definitions.
2. Policy amendment that explicitly allows **licensing of anonymized / aggregated trading telemetry**, while keeping “we do not sell personal information” and “we do not commercialize individual user strategies.”
3. Anonymization standard (k-anonymity, no rare EA signatures, time jitter if needed).
4. DPA / data-license agreement: purpose limitation, no re-identification, no sublicense, audit rights, deletion on termination.
5. Optional: checkout checkbox / terms notice for “anonymized research and model-training use.”

Until that is done, sell **distribution**, not data — or run a tiny paid pilot on a synthetic / heavily aggregated extract only.

## 5. Strategic fit with Verodus economics

Verodus makes money when people **buy evaluations** and, in large numbers, **fail or reset**, while payouts stay bounded. A partnership that simply “makes better traders” shrinks that machine.

MoniMath’s education / coaching / behavioral-improvement story is therefore **not automatically aligned**. It becomes aligned only if it is used as:

- a **volume channel** (more buyers, same rules), and/or
- a **premium upsell** (people pay extra for coaching; core challenge unchanged), and/or
- a **data sale** (cash independent of pass rates).

It is **misaligned** if Verodus:

- requires TradeMath modules before a challenge,
- lets them rewrite rules to raise pass rates,
- or measures success as “how many of their students get funded.”

Exposure without profit is affiliate traffic that chargebacks. Profit without exposure is a quiet data license. Verodus asked for both — so run **A + B** (data cash + originated volume), and only then consider C.

## 6. Suggested commercial architecture

### Layer A — Data license (primary cash, independent of failures)

**Product (three SKUs, sell in this order):**

1. **Lifecycle pack (cheapest, still useful):** anonymized account-level outcomes, drawdown envelope, days traded, breach reason, program type, account size band, calendar regime tags. No fills.
2. **Behavioral feature pack (what they actually want):** sizing CV, trade clustering, news-window flags, weekend/Friday behavior, consistency / best-day ratios, EA-vs-manual marker, session histogram. Still no raw tickets.
3. **Survival pack (premium, annual only after they pay for 1–2):** evaluation behavior vs later funded survival / payout / breach. This is the dataset that trains “who blows up after passing.” Price it as the crown jewel. Do not include it in a pilot.

**Illustrative pricing (starting numbers for negotiation, not a valuation):**

- Pilot (90 days, SKU 1 only, cap on rows): **USD 20k–35k**, credited against Year 1 if they convert.
- Year 1 license (SKU 1+2): **USD 96k–180k** (USD 8k–15k/month) with a volume ratchet as Verodus’s trader count grows.
- SKU 3 add-on: **+USD 50k–80k/year**.
- Alternative: lower cash + **8–12% of net revenue** of any MoniMath / TradeMath product that uses the dataset (fund, signals, academy, PropTrade). Harder to audit; only accept with inspection rights and a **minimum guarantee**.

**Contract must-haves:**

- Verodus **owns** the data; they get a **non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, purpose-limited license**.
- Purpose: train internal TAMS models; **not** to train a competing evaluation CRM, **not** to solicit Verodus traders, **not** to publish identifiable case studies.
- 24-month **non-compete on evaluation/prop products** in markets where they use the data — **or** they must run PropTrade exclusively on Verodus rails (Layer C).
- Audit rights, hash-level samples, and a kill switch.
- Most-favored-nation only if they pay the top tier; never give exclusive data for free.

### Layer B — Exposure / distribution (this is the “exposure” ask)

MoniMath’s useful asset is **origination in HK, Greater China, and Chinese-speaking APAC**, plus academy/championship language.

**Structure:**

- Unique referral / UTMs / coupon. Verodus tracks every checkout.
- They market “path to Verodus evaluation” or a co-branded Asia landing page.
- Verodus pays **15–25% of net challenge fees** on tagged purchases (after refunds, fraud, chargebacks).
- **No** payment on funded-account profit share (that would reward them for funded survival, which Verodus may not want).
- Optional: they get a public “Asia education partner” badge. Verodus gets named on their championship/academy pages. That is the brand exposure.

This **increases absolute failures** even if mix quality is slightly better, because it adds new buyers. That is the version of “partnership” that fits a fail-heavy model.

Protect CAC: 30-day cookie, no self-referrals, fraud clawback, and they cannot run paid ads on the Verodus brand name.

### Layer C — White-label the thing they are already trying to build

They announced PropTrade. They do not appear to have MT5/TradeHub, KYC, payout rails, or a rule engine. Two outcomes:

- **Good:** PropTrade is a skin on Verodus. Verodus keeps operations, risk, and 70–85% of GMV. They keep 15–30% for origination and local marketing. All traders stay in Verodus CRM (more data, more fees).
- **Bad:** they use Verodus data to specify a clone, then put it on a generic prop white-label (Brokeret / FXPropTech / etc.) and compete.

Make Layer C the price of Layer A if they want SKU 2–3. In plain language: *if you want our behavioral data, your prop product runs on our rails for 24 months.*

### Layer D — Education as a paid add-on only

Keep Instant / 1-Step / 2-Step exactly as they are.

Offer “TradeMath / TAMS coaching” as an optional SKU at checkout (USD 99–399). Split 50/50 or 60/40 Verodus. People who want better odds **pay extra**. People who do not, fail at the current rate.

Never gate funding on academy completion.

## 7. How this is financially beneficial for Verodus (with a fail-heavy model)

Worked illustration only. Replace with live CRM numbers before sending a term sheet.

Assume:

- Incremental Asia volume from them: 400 extra challenges / month at USD 180 blended net fee → **USD 72k/month GMV**.
- Affiliate payout 20% → Verodus keeps **USD 57.6k/month** on that flow. Most of those accounts still fail. Payout leakage stays bounded by existing rules.
- Data license: **USD 10k/month**.
- Education add-on attach 8% of their flow at USD 149, 50% share → ~USD 2.4k/month.

**Directional Year-1 Verodus take:** data ~USD 120k + distribution net ~USD 690k + upsell ~USD 29k, before extra support cost. Even if Asia volume is **one-quarter** of this, data + a thin affiliate stream still beats “free data for exposure.”

Compare to the bad deal: free data + co-branded education that raises pass rates 3–5 points on the **existing** book, with no volume guarantee. That can easily cost more in extra payouts than any brand value.

**Rule of thumb for the room:** every 1 percentage point increase in pass rate on the core book is a cost. Every incremental challenge sold at the current rule set is a profit. Price the partnership so volume and data cash dominate any quality effect.

- SFC Type 2 mislabeled.
- WordPress.com marketing site, 2026 vintage, template leftovers (“Linear simplifies software project management”).
- Jane Street name-drop; Rolex-metaphor championship with no event mechanics.

None of these kill a **paid, tightly scoped** deal. Together they kill a **strategic exclusive partnership** or any use of the Verodus brand on their fund.

### 8.3 Legal / reputational

- Unlicensed HK fund marketing. Do not let Verodus be named on MoniMath Capital materials until the licence is proven.
- China marketing (ICP, cross-border payments, “championship in provinces”) is a separate legal workstream. Do not green-light mainland campaigns from a slide.

- CCPA “sale” and GDPR anonymization standards are not the same as “the CRM vendor said we can sell data.”

### 8.4 Walk-away conditions

Stop if they:

- want a free or token-priced data dump “for partnership”;
- refuse to name the SFC counterparty after a reasonable diligence window;
- insist on exclusivity without a USD six-figure minimum;
- want raw tickets + identity keys;
- or stand up PropTrade on a third-party prop stack while still consuming Verodus data.

## 12. Bottom line

Joe Wong / MoniMath is a **distribution-and-narrative** counterparty who needs Verodus’s **data and rails** more than Verodus needs his brand.

The synergy is real, but it is not “we improve traders together.” The synergy that matches Verodus’s business is:

- **Sell the anonymized CRM behavioral data for cash.**
- **Sell him Asia-originated challenge volume on unchanged rules.**
- **If he wants a prop program, rent him Verodus, do not train his clone.**

That is exposure **and** profit. A free data-for-partnership deal is neither.
