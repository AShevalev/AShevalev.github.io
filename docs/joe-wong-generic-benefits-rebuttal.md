## What that memo gets wrong (kill)

The memo is written as if Verodus were a North American prop shop hunting an HK halo. Verodus is **Verodus L.L.C.-FZ** (Dubai), payments via Verodus Capital Inc., ~3,000 users, 175+ countries already. Joe cannot add a geography Verodus does not already sell into. He can only add **named buyers**, if they hit checkout with a code.

### 1. “Trader acquisition / lower CAC” off 10,000 traders and a 1,700-person Saudi event

**Category true, inputs false.**

- TradeMath About still claims 10k members, 10k asset classes, 10M+ backtests, 50+ institutional networks, a “Jane Street” framework — with the metric row **duplicated and mislabeled**. MoniMath LinkedIn posts still get ~3–5 reactions. That is not a 10k algo community.
- The 2025 “1,700 with the Saudi government” claim has no public TradeMath / Joe / World Traders Hub match. Nearest public shape is Algo Challenge Association at Money20/20 Riyadh; they are not on the sponsor list; prizes ~USD 2k.
- Championship on his site is a **Rolex essay + form** plus “City & Provincial” China language. That is not a pre-filtered challenge buyer. “Somewhat pre-filtered” is invented.
- CAC falls only if **unique-code** checkouts beat paid UA after refunds and chargebacks. Model 20 / 100 / 500 Instant at $49, not 10,000. Twenty tagged Instant checkouts in 90 days is ~$980 gross. That is a meeting that produced a list, not a channel.

Official partner status, co-branded promotion, and “preferred platform placement” with **no unique code and no net-fee share** is the unpaid logo he asked for. That raises CAC in disguise: you spend brand and support, he keeps the list.

### 2. “Asia / Hong Kong credibility signal”

**This is the trap, not the prize.**

- Verodus is not “building from North America.” It already sells worldwide from a Dubai FZ entity. An HK think-tank adjacency does not open a market Verodus lacks. It imports a claim Verodus would then own.
- Our Hong Kong Foundation is real (Tung Chee-hwa). No public page ties OHKF to Joe, TradeMath, MoniMath, or World Traders Hub HK. Using OHKF in Verodus copy without a letter is how a journalist or a regulator asks you to prove Tung Chee-hwa’s foundation stood up Joe’s competition.
- MoniMath Capital “SFC approval” is a 20 Nov 2025 LinkedIn post (~3 reactions). The live MoniMath site says **partners** with Types 1/2/9, mislabels Type 2 as “Advising on Securities” (that is Type 4), and names no CE number and no licensed corporation. LinkedIn “approval” vs site “partners” is a contradiction, not a positive data point.
- “Marketing claims around institutional-grade partnerships” is exactly what Verodus must not do. The wording brief already exists to stop that class of leak.

Intros to unnamed platforms, mentors, and capital networks are the same as the unnamed BofA / Crédit Lyonnais / RBC / KPMG “executive members.” Names or drop.

### 3. “Education and content leverage”

**Optional upsell only. Never a pathway.**

TradeMath’s Level 1–4 ladder is still duplicated/broken on the homepage. No named faculty. Integrating Verodus rules into that curriculum, or making the course a gate to Instant, does three things:

1. Kills Instant impulse purchase (the $49 / 35%-off motion).
2. Lets Joe capture the course fee while Verodus takes KYC, support, and payout load.
3. Raises pass rates if he coaches to the rules — which **hurts** fail-fee P&L if you also loosen anything for “the championship.”

Co-branded content is free advertising of his academy on your domain unless it ends in a tagged checkout. Reciprocal “access” is not a SKU.

### 4. “Competition and event branding”

**Common in the sector. Not free, and not evidenced here.**

Logo, speaking slots, and prize “funded accounts” are what he asked for with no cash and no tracking. For Verodus:

- Prize inventory = **capped Instant (or named 1-Step/Lite) evaluation accounts**, cost known, rules unchanged. Not live capital. Not uncapped 2-Step Pro.
- “Data on participant performance” is either his essay-form junk or **your** tickets / P&L / behavior. Live Privacy: trading-behavior data is not commercialized externally and not shared externally. Do not volunteer a feed “to make the partnership work.”
- A 2027 HK final with no venue, no CR number for World Traders Hub HK, and no OHKF letter is a landing page, not an event.

If he wants brand in HK, he **buys a cash sponsorship** with written OHKF/Saudi confirmation. He has not offered to buy it.
