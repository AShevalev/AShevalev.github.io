# Publishing sheet — hooks, captions, covers, CTAs, ad eligibility

One row per script. Scripts live in [`VIDEO-SCRIPTS.md`](VIDEO-SCRIPTS.md); timed cuts in [`REELS.md`](REELS.md); paid primary text in [`COPY.md`](COPY.md). This file decides **what runs as an ad, what stays organic, and what each one needs to convert.**

Two rules that shape everything below:

- **Paid destination is the bridge page, never checkout.** See the policy gate in [`CAMPAIGN.md`](CAMPAIGN.md).
- **Video VO carries no prices.** Organic captions may name the price. Paid primary text comes from `COPY.md`.

## Hook A/B bank — ad candidates only

First three seconds decide the spend. Shoot **two hooks per ad candidate as separate takes**, run them as separate ads, keep the winner. Same body either way, so it's one performance and two openers.

Kill under **25%** three-second hook rate. Scale over **45%**.

| # | Hook A — problem | Hook B — rule | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Most prop firms make traders feel trapped." | "Every objective is published before you pay. All of them." | A is emotional, B is proof-led. Expect A to win cold, B to win retarget. |
| 2 | "Some firms sell one rulebook and enforce another." | "The rules you start under are the rules you keep." | Third person in A — no *you* claim. |
| 4 | "Most challenges don't die on the profit target. They die on the floor." | "There are two kinds of drawdown, and one of them chases you." | A is the strongest hook in the pack. B is the safer educational read. |
| 5 | "Read the rules with me. Live page, no edits." | "Don't trust a firm's ad. Trust its objectives page." | B is sharper but implies competitor deception — keep it generic. |
| 7 | "This is the whole platform. Nothing edited out." | "You shouldn't have to pay to see how a firm works." | B is the best cold trial hook we have. |
| 8 | "This is the part where most firms start shouting." | "One code. We don't stack a second discount and call it a sale." | Warm only. |
| 11 | "Instant skips the evaluation. Here's the catch." | "No profit target. That's true — and it isn't the whole card." | Both lead with disclosure, which is why this one survives review. |
| 12 | "One phase. One set of numbers." | "No thirty-day clock. You pass on your schedule." | B tests better against FTMO-style time limits. |
| 13 | "Two kinds of floor. Only one stays put." | "Stopped out while you were still in profit? That's a trailing floor." | B is a question, not an assertion — keeps it clear of personal attributes. |
| C | "A one-step with every number published before you pay." | "Target, daily loss, max loss. Read them before you spend anything." | Straight rules read. |

**Never** open with a logo, a price, or "since May."

## Paid CTA buttons

Match the button to the destination or the click bounces.

| Campaign | Scripts | CTA button | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 — trial | 2, 7, 1 | **Sign Up** | Bridge → free trial |
| Week 2 — rules | 4, 5, 13 | **Learn More** | Bridge → objectives page |
| Week 3 — product | 11, 12, C | **Learn More** | Bridge → model card |
| Week 4 — offer, warm | 8, 10, 12 | **Shop Now** | Bridge → checkout |

**Learn More on all cold traffic.** "Shop Now" against a bridge page is a mismatch Meta reads as misleading, and it depresses CTR on audiences who don't know the brand yet. Warm buyers already know what they're clicking.

Never use **Get Offer** — it implies a coupon entitlement and pairs badly with a standing discount.

## Compliance checklist — run this on every ad before it goes live

Tied to the risks in `CAMPAIGN.md`. If any answer is wrong, the ad doesn't ship.

**Account level, once**

- [ ] Business verified, domain verified
- [ ] Financial-services authorisation held for **every** country in targeting
- [ ] **Financial products and services** Special Ad Category selected
- [ ] Age floor 18+
- [ ] Bridge page live, with risk + simulated disclosure above the fold
- [ ] Homepage earnings content (reward calculator, "$1M / 90%" headline, dollar certificates) not on the bridge page

**Per ad**

- [ ] Destination is the **bridge page**, not checkout
- [ ] No fee, size, or discount % spoken in the video
- [ ] No **crypto** rail mentioned in copy or visible in the creative
- [ ] No *you* statement about the viewer's losses or money — third person or a question instead
- [ ] No earnings figure, payout screenshot, or projection
- [ ] Any **static drawdown** claim carries a 2-Step label on screen and in VO
- [ ] Instant creative has **no** refund or static claim anywhere
- [ ] 90% presented as the **on-demand add-on**, never the standard split
- [ ] Payout speed reads "**most** rewards process in under 24 hours" — never "you get paid in 24 hours"
- [ ] No countdown or "ends tonight" unless the price actually changes
- [ ] Never the words "CFD trading" — this is a simulated evaluation
- [ ] Legal line burned into the final frame
- [ ] CTA button matches the destination (Learn More on cold)

**Weekly**

- [ ] Budget increases held to 20–30% every few days
- [ ] Comments moderated — delete income claims from other people on our ads
- [ ] Any rejection logged with the ad name before relaunching a variant
