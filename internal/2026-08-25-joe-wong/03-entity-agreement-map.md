## Software Sublicense and Data Licensing Agreement — 31 May 2026

**Parties:** 1591011 B.C. Ltd. (licensor) and Verodus L.L.C.-FZ (licensee).  
**Signatories:** Chun Chan, Director; Kim Chen, Manager.

- Limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable **licence** to install and use the Software for Meydan-authorised activities, including data collection, classification, analysis, and LLC-FZ’s own KYC.
- No modify, reverse-engineer, copy (except backup), or sublicense. LLC-FZ shall not sublicense to any third party.
- 1591011 retains all right, title, and interest in the Software. Improvements, configurations, and derivatives remain 1591011’s exclusive property.
- LLC-FZ performs all KYC itself and is not required to share KYC documents with 1591011. 1591011 is not LLC-FZ’s KYC provider.
- Raw Data, client-identifying information, and KYC records are exclusively LLC-FZ’s. LLC-FZ may commercialise raw Data, subject to law, but may not licence the Software itself.
- 1591011 receives a perpetual licence to Aggregated Insights only (anonymised outputs). That licence excludes raw Data, PII, and KYC records.
- 1591011 may host and process Data solely to operate the Software for LLC-FZ and to generate Aggregated Insights. It shall not sell personal Data or use it for its own marketing.
- Insights delivered with each six-month status report, and the complete set by the last day of the Term.
- No invoices and no cash. Reciprocal licences are the consideration.
- Term 36 months. On termination the software licence ends; the Aggregated Insights licence survives. 1591011 shall not retain KYC or raw PII after termination except as law requires.
- Independent contractors. No partnership, JV, fiduciary relationship, or agency.
- Governing law: British Columbia. Disputes: **VanIAC** (Vancouver International Arbitration Centre), Vancouver, English.

## Operational Services, Domain Usage and Evaluation Rights Agreement — 28 April 2026

**Parties:** Verodus Capital Inc. (service provider) and Verodus L.L.C.-FZ (client).  
**Signatories:** Kim Chen, Director (Capital); Kim Chen, Manager (LLC-FZ).

- LLC-FZ owns verodus.com and performs all KYC of Domain users in its own name. Capital does not provide KYC, does not collect identity documents for LLC-FZ, and is not LLC-FZ’s KYC provider, outsourced compliance function, or KYC processor.
- Capital’s service to LLC-FZ is marketing and traffic-driving to the Domain, including use of LLC-FZ’s affiliate software. Commissions from that activity are Capital’s to pay.
- Capital independently, for its own account: payment processing (pay-ins, rewards, refunds, banking) on merchant accounts in Capital’s name; platform evaluations solely for Capital’s own payment and reward decisions.
- Isolated merchant accounts. No commingling. Capital has no control of LLC-FZ’s merchant accounts and no access to LLC-FZ’s software except the affiliate-tool licence.
- LLC-FZ may give Capital only minimum user-status (for example verified / unverified / restricted) unless a processor or law requires more.
- Capital’s own payment-rail fraud, sanctions, and settlement checks are not KYC for LLC-FZ.
- Domain licence: non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable. Capital shall not sublicense it, shall not hold itself out as LLC-FZ except as needed to use the Domain, and shall not bind LLC-FZ.
- No invoices by default. LLC-FZ may, at sole discretion and on 90 days’ written notice, charge Capital a 5% royalty on gross sales (CAD), quarterly. Capital must keep records if that royalty is switched on.
- Subscription targets are planning metrics only. Missing them is not a material breach.
- Term 36 months. On termination the domain and evaluation licence ends; Capital stops using the Domain and affiliate software.
- Independent contractors. No partnership, JV, fiduciary relationship, or agency.
- Governing law: British Columbia. Disputes: **VanIAC**, Vancouver, English.

Both agreements may be amended in writing signed by both parties, and can be varied if this endeavour requires it.
