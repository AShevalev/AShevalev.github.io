How value moves

Software

1591011 and Holding each grant Dubai a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable sublicense. Dubai cannot re-sublicense to TradeMap, TAMS, or anyone else without a new grant from the owner.

Data

Raw book and PII stay with Dubai. Anonymised aggregates are licensed, perpetually, to 1591011 and Holding, who may commercialise those insights. Full delivery of aggregates is due within 36 months of each effective date.

Money

Capital processes receipts for its own account. No invoices by default. Dubai may switch on a 5% royalty of Capital gross sales (CAD) at its sole discretion, 90 days’ notice. Merchant accounts must stay isolated.
