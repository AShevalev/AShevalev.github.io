# Is Joe’s second-call package smart for Verodus?

**Internal.** Not for Joe. Pair with `18-second-call.md` and `19-huddle-after-second-call.md`.

**Verdict: the headline is usable. The package is not.** Take **$1.2 million for 60%** as a *strike to talk about*. Do not take 60/40 from signing, a delayed “for sure” buy after *his* volume, cash into the company instead of to the holders, a shell on the books, or free integration.

## What is actually good

- **$1.2 million for 60%** is above the huddle ask ($900,000) and above standing ($720,000). He moved off $1 million / $600,000.
- He will paper **all three companies together**. That matches the real perimeter (Capital + LLC-FZ + 1591011).
- He said the money, when it comes, is **cash**, not Hub as the price.
- B2B under World Trader Hub / TradeMap, B2C staying on Verodus for now, is a workable brand split.
- “You can make the requirement” is a small opening to write a **call**, not his standard delayed sale.

None of that makes the rest of the deal fair.

## Why the package fails Verodus

He wants majority **economics now**, majority **price frozen**, and majority **cash later** — after a $5 million sales path that is mostly his thesis.

**1. 60/40 from the day you sign, before he pays.**  
On the live book (ads off, leftover ~$44,500 a month) his 60% is ~$26,700 a month. Over 15 months that is about **$400,000** of Verodus profit to his side with no share cheque. On the normalised book (~$30,500 leftover) it is still about **$275,000**. That is larger than an integration deposit and in the same neighbourhood as an option premium. He is not funding growth. He is taking the existing engine.

**2. The freeze sits after *his* volume.**  
Milestone 3 is ~$5 million sales in 12–18 months. If that hits, 60% of a firm on that run-rate is worth several times $1.2 million. He said so himself ($10 million company → $6 million for 60%). Locking $1.2 million now is how he does not “pay more.” That is rational for him. It is a donation if it is a sale he must complete, or a free call if he can walk.

**3. “Inject into the company” is not a buyout.**  
If $1.2 million is primary, holders do not take $1.2 million out. They keep 40% of a company that just received his cash (about $480,000 of that cash economically) and have already given him 60% of the operating book. Alexander’s “one lump at milestone 3” assumed holders get paid. Joe’s words did not.

**4. Present value even if it *is* secondary.**  
$1.2 million in 15 months at a 25% cost of capital is about **$900,000 today** — the old ask — *before* subtracting 60% of interim profits and *before* giving him the upside to $5 million sales. Add those and the package is below the floor.

**5. No cash to start work.**  
You wanted an integration deposit and funds to grow. His labour-split is Kim (sole IT, live book) integrating TAMS for **$0**, after you floated **~$15,000**. He did not agree $15,000. He agreed each side pays its own people. That is free work on your stack.

**6. Shell on the books before 60% is paid.**  
A BC vehicle that takes bank, income, and cash management in milestone 1–2, then “buys” 60% at milestone 3, is control of receipts without paying for the companies. LLC-FZ holds user data and KYC. Capital holds the processor. Do not move those onto a Joe vehicle first.

**7. B2C may be shut or moved onto WTH once B2B works.**  
That is the current cash engine. It cannot be a side letter he can pull after 60/40 is already running.

**8. 40× / cannabis / 24–36 months.**  
Vision. Not a bid. Do not let it justify a cheap freeze.

**9. $500,000 sales was the wrong anchor.**  
Internal run-rate is ~$70,000 a month (~$840,000 a year). 5× that is **$4.2 million**, not $2.5 million. He used the figure you gave. Do not correct him upward in an email. Do not lock $2 million as if $500,000 were the diligence number. Kim should pull the dashboard before anyone papers a multiple.

**10. Cap table vs org chart.**  
Joe now has Kim 100% / Chun 100% *and* Kim 10% / Alexander 30% / Vincent 30% / Chun 30%. No contract is safe until one story is true.

## What “smarter for Verodus” looks like

Same headline, different instrument. Two packages; pick one in the huddle, send in writing after Friday, not at lunch.

### Package A — paid partnership, no equity freeze (best if you want cash to grow)

Leave the majority number until M&A, as he also offered in email.

| Term | Verodus position |
| --- | --- |
| Equity | No lock. No 60/40 on the existing book. |

| Grow-together cash | Monthly **$12,500–$15,000** for up to 6 months as a partnership retainer, **or** a 60/40 **only on new TradeMap / Hub B2B after costs**. Existing B2C stays 100% with current holders. If he stops paying, work and exclusivity stop. |

This is the only structure that matches “integration deposit and funds to grow together.”

### Package B — he wants a frozen $1.2 million (only as a call)

Take his **$1.2 million for 60% of all three** as the **strike**, not as a delayed SPA.

| Term | Verodus position |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument | **Call option.** He may buy. He may walk. Not “for sure we will buy.” Window **12 months**, not 12–18 into $5 million sales with no ratchet. |
| If the freeze runs past a successful trial into his volume | Strike **ratchets** (e.g. +$X per $Y of run-rate above $70k/month) **or** the window is short. Do not give a long freeze and a cheap strike. |
| Premium | **$75,000** one cheque, or **$15,000 × 6** as instalments. Credits to $1.2 million if he exercises. **You keep it if he walks.** Unpaid month = option lapses. |
| Integration | Separate deposit, **does not credit.** Same $50,000 / $35,000 band as A. |
| Profits before exercise | **100% existing book to current holders.** Optional 60/40 only on **new B2B after costs**, and it **ends** if he does not exercise. |
| $1.2 million at exercise | **Secondary cash to the holders** (or a written split: part to holders, part into the company). Do not let “inject” mean 60% for $0 out. |
| Perimeter | All three companies at exercise, same day. Kim stays. Chun signs 1591011. |
| Vehicle | Listing / HoldCo **after** exercise, not a shell on receipts in milestone 1–2. |
| 100% later | Separate deal at then-market. Not on this freeze. |

If he refuses a call and insists on a sale that must complete at $1.2 million after *his* $5 million year, that is a delayed SPA. Do not sign it. A walk right without a premium is a free call with extra steps.
