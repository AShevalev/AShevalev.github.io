# Verodus organisation structure

**As of 25 August 2026.** Operating companies only. Verodus Holding Inc. is a separate company and is **not** part of this structure.

There is **no parent** and **no subsidiary**. The three companies are **affiliates**. The individual signatory owns **100% of the shares** of each company personally. They deal with each other by contract, not by corporate control.

| Entity | Jurisdiction | Ownership | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1591011 B.C. Ltd.** | British Columbia, Canada | 100% individual signatory | Owns the proprietary software, CRM, and related technology. Sublicenses the system to Verodus L.L.C.-FZ. |
| **Verodus Capital Inc.** | British Columbia, Canada | 100% individual signatory | Profit centre. Payment processing on Canadian merchant accounts. Marketing, traffic, KYC support. Licensed to use `verodus.com`. |
| **Verodus L.L.C.-FZ** | Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, UAE | 100% individual signatory | Owns `verodus.com`. Owns raw data and client-identifying information. Runs the CRM and dashboard. |

## What is not on this chart

**Verodus Holding Inc.** is a separate company. It is out of the operating perimeter and is not a parent, subsidiary, or affiliate on this chart.

## Who holds what

| Question | Current answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who owns each company? | The individual signatory owns **100%** of each of the three companies. No corporate parent. |
| Who owns the domain? | Verodus L.L.C.-FZ (`verodus.com`). |
| Who holds the data? | Verodus L.L.C.-FZ owns **raw** data and client-identifying information. Aggregated insights are licensed to 1591011 B.C. Ltd. |
| Who owns the system? | **1591011 B.C. Ltd.** Dubai’s right is a limited, revocable, non-transferable sublicense. Dubai cannot re-sublicense. |
| Where does money land? | **Verodus Capital Inc.** A UAE processor exists but is not used. |
| How are the three related? | Affiliates under the same individual shareholder. Contracts between them; no parent–subsidiary. |

## Intercompany contracts still in force between these three

- Software sublicense (31 May 2026): 1591011 B.C. Ltd. ↔ Verodus L.L.C.-FZ. No cash.
- Operational services, domain usage and evaluation rights (28 April 2026): Verodus Capital Inc. ↔ Verodus L.L.C.-FZ. No invoices by default. Dubai may charge a **5% royalty** on Capital gross sales (CAD) at its discretion, 90 days’ notice.

The April 2026 software sublicense that named Verodus Holding Inc. is **outside** this operating chart.
