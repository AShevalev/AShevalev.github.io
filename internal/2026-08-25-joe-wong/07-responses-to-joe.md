## Product, risk, and results

**Same challenge models, or redesigned for Hong Kong?**  
Start from what we run today, then we adjust together — account size, drawdown, and price. Hong Kong tickets can start a little higher if income levels warrant it. I can send the pricing catalogue with the challenge parameters.

**What are the models, sizes, and risk rules today?**  
Four evaluation types: Instant, one-step, two-step Lite, two-step Pro.

- Instant: five sizes, $5k–$100k. No minimum trading days. 20% best-day rule. Trailing drawdown. Harder to pass; attracts gamblers.
- One-step: six sizes, $5k–$200k. 50% best-day rule (in practice about three trading days). Trailing drawdown. Largest revenue source; amateur / retail.
- Two-step Pro: 5% daily / 10% max drawdown. Phase 2 profit target 5%. Professionals tend to sit here. No trailing drawdown. Better payouts.
- Two-step Lite: 4% daily / 8% max. Phase 2 profit target 4%.

We removed the news-trading rule; it was not needed for this book.

**How do you control risk? What is the mix?**  
Rules and drawdowns on the account, not extra trader-level caps. One-step is the main revenue line. Instant is difficult to pass. Consistent traders go to the two-steps.

**How many ongoing winners, and how long do they last?**  
Less than 2% ongoing winners. Some funded accounts have lasted about 70 days. Only two or three accounts have reached a third payout. About 60 payouts in four months, roughly 15 a month, on about 3,000 traders.

**What do they trade?**  
Mostly gold / metals. Some crypto.
