# What changed in these revised drafts

Effective dates are unchanged (28 April 2026 and 31 May 2026). The documents are revised as of 25 August 2026.

## Both agreements

- Reciprocal rights remain the consideration. There is still no invoice process and no separate price for each right or service.
- Removed the recital that the barter “reflects fair value on an arm’s-length basis, consistent with transfer pricing regulations.” That sentence was a conclusion without a pricing file. The contracts now say only that the parties have determined the reciprocal grants are sufficient consideration.
- Dispute resolution cites VanIAC (the forum’s name since 2020), not BCICAC.
- Independence language no longer claims the contract is “entirely independent of any other agreement.” It now says there is no partnership, joint venture, or agency, and that the contract does not amend merchant-processor agreements.

## Operational Services Agreement

- LLC-FZ does all KYC of Domain users itself.
- Capital is not LLC-FZ’s KYC provider and does not collect identity documents for LLC-FZ.
- Capital may still do payment-rail, fraud, sanctions, and processor checks on its own merchant accounts. That is Capital’s own compliance, not KYC for LLC-FZ.
- LLC-FZ may pass Capital only a user status (verified / unverified / restricted) unless a processor or law requires more.
- Capital’s service to LLC-FZ is marketing and traffic-driving only. Administrative / KYC support is gone.
- The optional 5% gross-sales royalty is kept as the only monetary exception. The contract states it is not a price for any individual leg.
- Subscription targets are planning figures only and do not create a fee.
- Overlapping management is acknowledged. Signature blocks are unchanged.

## Software License and Data Licensing Agreement

- Retitled from “sublicense” to “license.” Recital A already says 1591011 B.C. LTD. owns the Software, so the grant is a licence from the owner.
- LLC-FZ may use the Software for its own KYC. KYC records stay LLC-FZ property and are not licensed back.
- Aggregated Insights are delivered with each six-month report, and in full by the end of the Term, instead of only at month 36.
- The licence back remains royalty-free and unpriced. Raw data and KYC files are excluded from it.
